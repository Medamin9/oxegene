## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an upscale, contemporary specialty coffee and twilight lounge experience. It bridges daytime precision brewing with an evening social atmosphere, capturing the ritual of craft beverages through moody, ambient environments punctuated by electric illumination.

The design movement synthesizes **Tactile Glassmorphism** with **Nocturnal Minimalism**:
- **Tactile Glassmorphism:** Translucent frosted panes, delicate chromatic borders, and controlled atmospheric glows provide a sense of physical layering without visual clutter.
- **Deep Immersion:** Saturated midnight bases allow vibrant ultraviolet and soft lavender accents to direct eye flow to menus, pairings, and checkout triggers.
- **Sculpted Geometry:** Generous pill forms evoke the smooth contours of polished ceramics and streamlined architectural bar counters, producing a fluid, hospitable digital touch surface.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, nocturnal canvas with vibrant focal points:

- **Primary (`#7C3AED` - Electric Violet):** Drives key calls to action, active toggles, highlighted tasting notes, and primary transactional triggers.
- **Secondary (`#A78BFA` - Soft Lavender):** Used for supporting indicators, subheadings, chip badges, and subtle linear gradient stops.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Cyan Mist):** Reserved for technical metrics such as roast profiles, brew temperatures, origin elevations, and live cart notification indicators.
- **Neutral (`#090D16` - Deep Midnight):** The foundational dark base. Tinted with deep blue-slate undertones rather than pure black to preserve atmospheric warmth.

### Functional Roles & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#090D16` (Deep Midnight)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Container):** `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.70)` with a `1px` inner border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Modal/Drawer):** `rgba(24, 32, 51, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `20px`
- **Text Primary:** `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Off-White, 98% opacity)
- **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8` (Cool Slate Gray, 75% opacity)
- **Text Tertiary:** `#64748B` (Muted Charcoal/Slate, 50% opacity)
- **Success / In-Stock:** `#10B981`
- **Attention / Limited Batch:** `#F59E0B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric clarity of **Outfit** for structural titles with the legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and metadata.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Geometric cuts and generous apertures provide modern presence without feeling sterile. Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on larger scales gives menu items and section titles a tailored editorial balance.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its rounded letter terminals echo the pill geometry of the interface components, ensuring comfortable reading across drink descriptions, tasting notes, and nutritional breakdowns.
- **Micro-Labels:** Use uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) on `label-md` and `label-sm` for roast types, origin markers, and price tags to create clear hierarchy against conversational item titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid configured within a responsive 12-column architecture (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop).

- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Outer canvas margins compress from `3rem` on desktop to `1rem` on mobile, maximizing space for thumb-friendly selection grids.
- **Menu Matrix Reflow:** Product cards follow a single-column layout on mobile (`< 640px`), expanding to 2 columns on tablet (`640px - 1024px`), and 3 or 4 columns on desktop views (`> 1024px`).
- **Cart & Customization Panels:** Modals and bottom sheets lock to edge boundaries on handheld screens with a maximum width of `480px` centered on larger devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass layering and violet-tinted backdrops rather than conventional high-opacity drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#090D16` baseline tone.
- **Level 1 (Menu Tiles & Cards):** Background `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Bars & Sticky Filters):** Background `rgba(24, 32, 51, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.20)`. Shadow: `0 12px 40px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.60), 0 0 20px 2px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Cart Drawer, Modals, Popovers):** Background `rgba(15, 20, 32, 0.90)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.80), 0 0 40px -8px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a continuous pill and capsule silhouette across interactive touchpoints:

- **Buttons & Tags:** Strictly fully-rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to reinforce the hospitality and café identity.
- **Containers & Glass Cards:** Follow large pill-adjacent radii (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `rounded-3xl` / `2rem`), softening the technical dark-mode structure.
- **Form Controls:** Full-radius pill inputs for search, counters, and quantity steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Order", "Checkout"):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), background `#7C3AED`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Glow: `0 0 24px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.40)`. On hover: background shifts to `#6D28D9` with increased ambient glow.
- **Secondary (e.g., "Details", "Quick View"):** Full pill, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.30)`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost/Icon Button:** Translucent circle (`rounded-full`), `44px x 44px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, hover background `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tasting / Origin Chips:** Height `28px`, full pill, padding `0 12px`, background `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`, text `#A78BFA`, font `label-sm`.
- **Filter Pills (Interactive):** Unselected state uses `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` border and muted text; selected state toggles to solid `#7C3AED` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards (Product & Tasting Profiles)
- Padding `space-lg`, glass base (`rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.65)`), `border-radius: 1.5rem`.
- Features an image container with rounded upper geometry (`1.25rem`), price rendered in `Outfit` medium, and an integrated quick-add circular button anchored in the bottom-right corner.

### Form Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Search & Text Input:** Height `48px`, full pill shape, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`. Focus ring: `0 0 0 2px #7C3AED`.
- **Steppers (Milk, Sweetener, Shots):** Capsule enclosure housing minus and plus icon buttons, separated by centered numeric text in `label-lg`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Radio / Options:** Pill segments arranged horizontally. Active item slides into an illuminated capsule with `background: #7C3AED` and text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded squares (`rounded-md`), border `1.5px solid rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.40)`, transitioning to `#7C3AED` fill with an off-white checkmark on selection.

### Cart Drawer & Product Customizer Modal
- **Cart Drawer:** Slips in from the right viewport boundary on desktop; bottom-sheet on mobile. Uses `Level 3` elevation with frosted glass backdrop blur. Footer contains an order summary line and a persistent sticky primary checkout pill.
- **Customizer Modal:** Centered dialog framed with an electric violet ambient glow. Groups options (Milk Type, Temperature, Syrups, Ice Level) into distinct horizontal pill selector rows with real-time price updates.
